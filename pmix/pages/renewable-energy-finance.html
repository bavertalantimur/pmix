Renewable Energy Finance

pmgix supports the development, financing, and execution of renewable energy and energy transition projects, including solar, wind, storage, biomass, energy efficiency, and related infrastructure.

Our work focuses on helping clients structure projects that are financially robust, bankable, scalable, and attractive to lenders and investors.

Key capabilities include:

  • Renewable energy project finance advisory
  • Power project bankability assessment
  • Revenue model and offtake analysis
  • Tariff and cash flow modeling
  • Sponsor and lender engagement support
  • Risk allocation and contract review coordination
  • Investment case preparation
  • Financing structures for clean energy projects

Value delivered: pmgix helps clients move renewable energy projects from concept to financing readiness by aligning capital, risk, stakeholders, and execution strategy.
