Financial Modeling

pmgix develops and reviews financial models that support investment decisions, financing processes, project development, corporate planning, valuation, restructuring, and portfolio monitoring.

Our models are designed to be transparent, flexible, auditable, and decision-focused.

Key capabilities include:

  • Project finance models
  • Corporate financial models
  • Valuation models
  • Debt sizing and repayment models
  • Scenario and sensitivity analysis
  • Cash flow forecasting
  • Budget and business plan models
  • Investment return analysis
  • Portfolio monitoring dashboards

Value delivered: pmgix helps clients make better decisions by providing clear, reliable, and commercially relevant financial analysis that reflects real-world financing and investment requirements.
