Workshops

Focused, hands-on sessions for teams that need practical skills quickly — from lender-grade models to structuring choices ahead of financial close.

Workshops are designed for immediacy: a defined problem, a working session, and outputs your team can use. Led by pmgix practitioners, each workshop combines instruction with applied exercises drawn from project finance and transaction advisory.

Workshop formats:

  • Financial Modeling Intensives — build, stress-test, and present decision-ready models
  • Deal Structuring Clinics — senior and subordinated debt, equity, and risk allocation
  • Due Diligence Sessions — commercial, financial, and documentation readiness
  • Scenario & Sensitivity Masterclasses — underwriting discipline for lenders and boards

Who it is for: Project teams preparing for mandate milestones, financing rounds, or lender presentations — when depth matters more than breadth, and time is limited.
