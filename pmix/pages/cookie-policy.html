1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function properly, remember preferences, and understand how visitors use online content. Similar technologies — such as local storage, pixels, and tags — may serve comparable purposes. In this policy, we refer to all of these collectively as “cookies” unless otherwise stated.

Cookies may be:

  • Session cookies — deleted when you close your browser;
  • Persistent cookies — remain on your device for a defined period or until deleted;
  • First-party cookies — set by pmgix;
  • Third-party cookies — set by external providers whose services appear on our site.

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies to:

  • enable core website functionality and security;
  • remember choices you make, such as cookie consent preferences;
  • understand how visitors navigate and interact with our pages;
  • measure the effectiveness of content and campaigns where analytics tools are enabled;
  • improve performance, accessibility, and user experience.

Where required by law — including under the GDPR, UK GDPR, and ePrivacy rules — we obtain your consent before placing non-essential cookies on your device.

3. Types of cookies we use

Category Purpose Consent required
Strictly necessary Security, load balancing, navigation, and storage of cookie preferences No — required for site operation
Functional Remembering settings such as language or form state Yes, where mandated by law
Analytics Aggregated statistics on page visits, traffic sources, and site performance Yes
Marketing Measuring campaign reach and relevance of promotional content Yes

We review our cookie inventory periodically and update this table when technologies change. Specific cookie names and durations may vary by deployment environment.

4. Third-party technologies

Some cookies are placed by service providers that support our website operations. These may include hosting infrastructure, font delivery networks, analytics platforms, or embedded media providers. Third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies.

We aim to configure third-party tools with privacy-respecting settings, such as IP anonymisation where available, and limit data collection to what is necessary for the stated purpose.

5. Managing your preferences

You can control cookies in several ways:

  • Cookie banner or settings panel — when displayed on our site, use it to accept or reject non-essential cookies or adjust categories.
  • Browser controls — most browsers let you block, delete, or limit cookies. Visit your browser’s help section for instructions.
  • Device settings — mobile operating systems may provide additional tracking controls.
  • Industry opt-out tools — certain analytics and advertising networks offer centralised opt-out mechanisms.

Please note that disabling strictly necessary cookies may affect website functionality. Blocking analytics cookies will not prevent you from using the site, but it limits our ability to improve content based on usage patterns.

6. Updates to this policy

We may revise this Cookie Policy to reflect changes in technology, regulation, or our practices. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

7. Contact us

If you have questions about our use of cookies or wish to exercise privacy rights relating to data collected through cookies, contact privacy@pmgix.com.
