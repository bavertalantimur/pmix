Certifications

Structured credentials that recognize applied competence in financial modeling, project finance, and transaction advisory — grounded in pmgix delivery standards.

Certifications provide a clear pathway from fundamentals to practitioner-level execution. Each program combines instruction, applied exercises, and assessment tied to real advisory work — helping professionals and organizations establish a shared baseline of quality and credibility.

Credential pathways:

  • Financial Modeling — lender-grade models, sensitivities, and documentation
  • Project Finance Practitioner — structures, bankability, and stakeholder materials
  • Transaction Advisory Foundations — diligence, valuation, and deal execution
  • Continuing Development — refresher modules as markets and regulation evolve

Who it is for: Analysts, associates, and mid-career professionals seeking recognized capability in finance-intensive advisory — and employers building consistent technical standards.
