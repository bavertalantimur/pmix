Executive Finance

pmgix provides senior finance advisory support for companies requiring strategic financial leadership, stronger financial discipline, improved reporting, or better alignment between finance and business strategy.

This service is particularly relevant for growing companies, project sponsors, investment platforms, family businesses, and organizations preparing for financing, M&A, restructuring, or expansion.

Key capabilities include:

  • CFO-level advisory
  • Financial planning and analysis
  • Budgeting and forecasting
  • Cash flow and liquidity management
  • Finance function improvement
  • Board and investor reporting
  • KPI and dashboard design
  • Internal controls and governance support
  • Interim or fractional CFO support

Value delivered: pmgix helps clients strengthen financial leadership, improve planning discipline, enhance investor readiness, and build more effective finance functions.
