Transaction Advisory

pmgix provides transaction advisory support to help clients evaluate opportunities, identify risks, prepare investment decisions, and execute transactions with greater confidence.

We support buyers, sellers, sponsors, investors, lenders, and management teams throughout transaction processes.

Key capabilities include:

  • Financial due diligence support
  • Commercial and strategic review
  • Quality of earnings analysis support
  • Debt and working capital analysis
  • Risk identification and mitigation
  • Data room and information review support
  • Investment committee materials
  • Transaction readiness assessment
  • Deal execution support

Value delivered: pmgix helps clients reduce transaction risk, improve decision quality, identify value drivers, and approach transactions with stronger analytical and commercial confidence.
