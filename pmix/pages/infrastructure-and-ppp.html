Infrastructure & PPP

pmgix advises clients on infrastructure and public-private partnership projects requiring long-term planning, disciplined financial structuring, stakeholder coordination, and execution readiness.

This service is relevant for transport, utilities, logistics, social infrastructure, public assets, industrial infrastructure, and large-scale development projects.

Key capabilities include:

  • Infrastructure project feasibility assessment
  • PPP financial structuring
  • Concession and revenue model analysis
  • Public and private stakeholder coordination
  • Risk allocation analysis
  • Long-term project cash flow modeling
  • Financing strategy and lender preparation
  • Project lifecycle advisory

Value delivered: pmgix helps clients structure infrastructure and PPP projects with stronger financial discipline, clearer stakeholder alignment, and improved execution readiness.
