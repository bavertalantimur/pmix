Corporate Finance & M&A

pmgix provides corporate finance and M&A advisory support for clients evaluating acquisitions, disposals, partnerships, fundraising opportunities, joint ventures, and strategic transactions.

Our work combines valuation discipline, transaction analysis, negotiation support, and strategic positioning to help clients make informed decisions and execute value-creating transactions.

Key capabilities include:

  • Buy-side and sell-side advisory
  • M&A strategy and transaction planning
  • Valuation and pricing analysis
  • Acquisition and disposal assessment
  • Investor and shareholder materials
  • Deal structuring support
  • Due diligence coordination
  • Negotiation and closing support
  • Post-transaction considerations

Value delivered: pmgix helps clients assess transactions with clarity, reduce execution risk, improve negotiation readiness, and pursue opportunities that support long-term value creation.
