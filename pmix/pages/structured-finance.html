Structured Finance

pmgix supports clients in designing tailored financing solutions for transactions and projects that require more advanced capital structures than standard corporate lending.

This service is particularly relevant for projects, assets, or companies with complex cash flow profiles, multi-party financing needs, refinancing requirements, or hybrid debt and equity structures.

Key capabilities include:

  • Structured lending solutions
  • Asset-backed and cash flow-based financing
  • Hybrid debt and equity structuring
  • Refinancing and recapitalization strategies
  • Covenant and security package analysis
  • Capital stack assessment
  • Lender and investor positioning
  • Financing term optimization

Value delivered: pmgix helps clients access capital more effectively by creating financing structures that are commercially credible, risk-adjusted, and aligned with lender and investor expectations.
