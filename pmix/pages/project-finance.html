Project Finance

pmgix advises clients on the structuring and financing of capital-intensive projects across energy, infrastructure, industrial assets, logistics, real assets, technology-enabled infrastructure, and transition-related investments.

Our project finance work focuses on turning project opportunities into bankable, finance-ready structures by aligning commercial, technical, financial, legal, and stakeholder requirements.

Key capabilities include:

  • Project feasibility and bankability assessment
  • Capital structure design
  • Debt sizing and repayment capacity analysis
  • Project cash flow forecasting
  • Sponsor, lender, and investor alignment
  • Risk allocation and mitigation planning
  • Financial close preparation
  • Term sheet and financing documentation support
  • Lender presentation materials

Value delivered: pmgix helps clients improve project bankability, reduce financing uncertainty, and prepare projects for successful lender, investor, and stakeholder engagement.
