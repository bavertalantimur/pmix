Executive Education

Programs for boards, C-suite leaders, and senior executives who allocate capital, govern major projects, and steer organizations through financing and transformation.

Executive Education at pmgix Academy brings the judgment of our advisory practice into focused learning experiences. Drawing on project finance, corporate finance, and restructuring mandates, we help leaders strengthen decision-making on bankability, stakeholder alignment, and capital deployment — without unnecessary complexity.

Program focus:

  • Capital Strategy — allocation, portfolio priorities, and financing trade-offs
  • Board & Governance — oversight of major projects and lender relationships
  • Transformation Leadership — restructuring, turnaround, and growth financing
  • Stakeholder Confidence — sponsors, investors, governments, and operators
  • Cross-Border Growth — market entry, partnerships, and risk positioning

Who it is for: CEOs, CFOs, board members, and senior leaders responsible for capital-intensive investments, public-private programs, or enterprise-wide financial transformation.
