Legal & Documentation

pmgix supports clients by coordinating legal, contractual, and documentation-related workstreams that are critical to financing, investment, M&A, restructuring, and project execution processes.

pmgix does not act as a law firm or provide formal legal opinions. Instead, we work alongside legal counsel, tax advisors, lenders, sponsors, and transaction parties to help ensure that legal documentation is aligned with the financial structure, commercial objectives, and execution timeline.

Key capabilities include:

  • Coordination with external legal counsel
  • Review support for financing documentation
  • Term sheet and mandate letter support
  • Transaction documentation coordination
  • Contractual risk identification from a financial perspective
  • Support for shareholder, lender, and investor documentation
  • Legal due diligence coordination
  • Closing checklist and documentation process management
  • Alignment of legal terms with financial model assumptions

Value delivered: pmgix helps clients align legal documentation, financial structures, risk allocation, and transaction execution throughout the advisory process.
