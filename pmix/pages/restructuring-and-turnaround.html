Restructuring & Turnaround

pmgix supports clients facing liquidity pressure, debt challenges, operational underperformance, capital structure constraints, or complex stakeholder negotiations.

Our restructuring and turnaround work focuses on stabilizing the business, improving financial visibility, and creating a credible path toward recovery or repositioning.

Key capabilities include:

  • Financial diagnostics
  • Liquidity and cash flow analysis
  • Debt restructuring support
  • Capital structure review
  • Turnaround planning
  • Working capital improvement
  • Stakeholder negotiation support
  • Recovery strategy and implementation roadmap
  • Interim finance leadership support

Value delivered: pmgix helps clients regain control, improve financial resilience, manage stakeholder expectations, and develop credible restructuring plans.
