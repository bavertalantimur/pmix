Corporate Training

Bespoke programs that build consistent capability across finance, legal, and project teams — aligned to your mandates, sectors, and delivery standards.

Corporate Training translates pmgix advisory methods into practical team learning. We design curricula around your live priorities — whether preparing for financial close, standardizing models, or strengthening transaction readiness — and deliver them on-site, virtually, or in blended formats.

Typical modules:

  • Project Finance Essentials — structures, cash flows, and lender expectations
  • Financial Modeling Standards — audit-ready models and scenario discipline
  • Transaction Readiness — diligence, data rooms, and investor materials
  • Risk & Governance — controls, compliance, and reporting for capital providers
  • Custom Curriculum — sector-specific content shaped to your organization

Who it is for: Corporate finance, project development, legal, and strategy teams at sponsors, developers, lenders, and public-sector entities running capital programs.
